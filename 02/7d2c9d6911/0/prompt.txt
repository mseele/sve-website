This ticket resolves two open design questions and applies the answer — it is both a decision and its implementation.

Decision 1 (B1 — overloaded field): The field `availableSlots` on `EventAvailability` does two domain jobs. When `isWaitingList` is false it means "remaining subscriber slots"; when true it means "remaining waiting-list slots." Decide: is this a deliberate phase-switched capacity (keep the field, name and document the switch), or two conflated quantities (split into `remainingSubscriberSlots` + `remainingWaitingListSlots`)?

Decision 2 (B2 — consumer sentinel decode): The `-1` contract spans four sites — `convertToEventAvailability` (write), `calculateAvailabilityMessage` (read), the `<script>` in `src/components/blocks/Event.astro` around the availability card (read `=== -1`), and `src/components/blocks/Events.astro` (read `=== 0`). The two `.astro` consumer sites are not exported TS and unit tests cannot reach them. Decide: extract the consumer decoders into exported pure predicates (e.g. `canSubscribe(av)`, `availabilityState(av)`), or accept that the `.astro` sites are only covered by the build + a later Playwright pass?

Record both decisions in `CONTEXT.md` as resolved domain terms, and create an ADR in `docs/adr/` if either qualifies (hard to reverse + surprising + real trade-off).

The goal: the entire `-1` contract is unit-testable, and a future sentinel refactor (e.g. to `Infinity` or a `kind: 'unlimited'`) either moves all four sites together or fails a test.
